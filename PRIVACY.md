# Privacy

Hurtly is built so your health information stays yours.

## What's stored, and where

Everything you log (painted areas, intensity, descriptions, notes, answers to follow-up questions) is stored **in your browser on your device**, using the browser's built-in storage (IndexedDB). Settings, including a Gemini key if you add one, are stored in the browser too.

There is no Hurtly account and no Hurtly server. We never receive, see or store your entries.

- **Clearing your browser's site data deletes your entries.** Use **Settings → Export backup** to keep a copy.
- **Delete all entries** in Settings removes everything from the device.

## AI features

AI is off until you turn it on in Settings.

- **On this device:** your text is processed by the model built into your browser. It doesn't leave your device.
- **Gemini with your own key:** when you use an AI feature, the text you typed and the related diary entries are sent from your device directly to Google's Gemini API under your own key. Google's terms for that key apply. Nothing passes through Hurtly.

Hurtly sends AI only what a feature needs: your description, the entries for the same body areas, or the report summary you asked for.

## Other connections

- **Fonts** are loaded from Google Fonts.
- **Hosting:** the app is served as a static website. The host may keep standard request logs (such as IP addresses), as any website host does. Your diary is never part of those requests.
- **No analytics, ads or tracking.**

## Changes

Changes to this policy are made in the open, in this file's history.
